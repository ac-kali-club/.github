## Hi there 👋
AC Kali Club is short for Algonquin College Kali Club.
The club reports to the [Algonquin College Student Association](https://www.algonquinsa.com) and is mostly ran by students and Alumni from [Algonquin College](https://www.algonquincollege.com)

The AC Kali club is the college's offensive cybersecurity club.
